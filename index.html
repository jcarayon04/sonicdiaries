About Sonic Diaries

Sonic Diaries is the instrumental guitar project of Julien Carayon — a self-taught guitarist with 30 years of experience, blending progressive rock, fusion, and modal harmony into cinematic compositions. Every note in this songbook has been transcribed and validated by the composer himself.
